Full Description

Built in 2020 by Ryder Homes, 24 Cottage Ln pairs classic craftsman charm with the ease of a nearly new home. A covered front porch with a cheerful red door opens to a bright, open living area with wood-look floors, big windows and a natural flow into the dining nook and kitchen.

The kitchen is finished in white shaker cabinetry, subway tile and stainless appliances, with plenty of room to cook and gather. The primary suite is on the main level, with a walk-in closet and a bath with a double vanity and walk-in shower, so day-to-day living can happen on one floor. Upstairs, two bedrooms with vaulted ceilings and a full bath make easy space for guests, hobbies or an office.

Central heat and A/C, smart home features, an inside laundry room with washer and dryer included, and a detached two-car garage round out the home. Hamilton Cottages is a 62+ community with landscaped common areas, a clubhouse, pergola-shaded gathering spaces and a fire pit courtyard just steps away.

Location

Hamilton sits on Novato's south side, where the historic Spanish-style buildings of the former Hamilton Field share the neighborhood with newer homes, parks and trails. You're close to the Hamilton Community Center, the restored wetlands along the bay, and quick Highway 101 access to the rest of Marin and San Francisco.
